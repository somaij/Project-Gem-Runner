CRYSTAL ISLES

The rules

  • Find every gem and Portal Magic. The clock stops when you have them all.
  • Gems lie about the islands, hide in chests (hit one to open it) and drop from enemies.
  • Portal Magic sits in crystal cages: beat the enemies guarding one and it opens.
  • Every enemy falls to one hit of your staff. Running into them doesn't count: attack.
  • Armored enemies only break at top speed, when speed lines trail behind you. Find a slope.
  • You have three hearts. A hit or a fall costs one, and you go back to the last checkpoint keeping everything you've found.
  • The pause menu shows how many gems and Portal Magic are left.
